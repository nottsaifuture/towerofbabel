THE TOWER OF BABEL — INTERACTIVE PRESENTATION

Open "Tower of Babel Interactive.html" in Chrome, Edge or Safari.
The file contains the model and viewer. No installation or internet connection is required.
Send the ZIP to other people or put it on a USB drive. Recipients should extract it and open the HTML file in their browser.

Drag to rotate and scroll/pinch to zoom.
Use Show to reveal a cutaway. Top view shows the ground floor from above.
Go inside selects room viewpoints. Drag to look; W A S D or arrow keys move. Q and E move down and up. Touch screens have direction buttons.
Open doors animates the hinged doors. Whole house restores the exterior view.
Full screen is useful for presenting or sharing your screen.

The browser model uses simpler materials than the renders. Upper-floor alignment and stairs remain provisional. Free view has no wall collision. This presentation is not the Unreal playable build.
